The short version

This is a one-person home bakery, not a company with a marketing department. The website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no account system. We ask for a name, an email address and your order — enough to bake the right thing and tell you when it's ready — and we don't sell, rent or share any of it with anyone.

What the website itself does

This site is a set of static files. There is no database behind it and no server that belongs to us. Specifically:

  • No cookies. Nothing is stored on your device by this site.
  • No analytics or tracking. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no session recording, no advertising tag.
  • No third-party embeds. The fonts are hosted on this site rather than loaded from a font network, so your visit isn't reported to a font provider. There are no embedded maps, videos or social widgets.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to store.

The only things that leave your browser are the page files themselves, and anything you deliberately choose to send using the buttons on the order form.

There is one thing the site writes down, and it is in plain sight: as you build a box, your choices are added to the page's own web address, so you can bookmark it or send it to someone and have the box come back. That address holds the box size, quantity and flavors — never your name, email or phone number. It is not saved to your device, and if you share the link, the only thing you are sharing is which bread you like.

What you send when you order

The order form builds a ticket containing:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to give one — it's optional
  • Your order: box size, quantity, flavors, and the pickup date
  • Anything you type into the notes field, which may include allergy or dietary information

That's the whole list. We never ask for a home address, a date of birth, or any payment card or bank details.

How your order actually reaches us

This part is unusual enough to be worth spelling out, because it means the website never holds your details even for a moment.

Filling in the form changes nothing outside your own browser. When you press Send order by email, your device opens your own email app with the ticket already written out, addressed to us. You are the one who sends it, from your own account, and you can edit or abandon it first. When you press Copy for Instagram DM, the ticket is copied to your clipboard so you can paste it into a message.

Either way, your order reaches us as an ordinary email or direct message. It is never posted to a server of ours, because there isn't one.

Payments

Boxes are paid for in advance by Cash App or Zelle. No payment is taken on this website, and no card or bank details are ever entered here or seen by us.

When you pay, that transaction happens inside Cash App or Zelle, under their own terms and privacy policies. What reaches us is what those apps show the recipient of a payment: typically the display name or handle on your account, the amount, the date, and whatever you write in the payment note. We use that only to match the payment to your order, which is why we ask you to put your name and pickup date in the note.

If you cancel before the Wednesday cutoff, the refund is sent back through the same app you paid with.

What we do with it

We use your details to:

  • Bake the right order and match your payment to it
  • Reply to confirm the order, and tell you if something has changed
  • Answer a question about an allergy or a special request
  • Keep a basic record of what was sold, for our own accounts and tax

We don't use it for anything else. There is no mailing list unless you ask to be told when a new flavor appears, and if you do ask, you can be taken off it by replying to any message. Nothing is sold, rented or handed to advertisers, data brokers or anyone else, and we don't build a profile of you.

How long we keep it

Order messages sit in the email inbox or the Instagram message thread they arrived in. Once an order has been collected and paid for, we keep the message for as long as it is useful to remember a repeat customer's usual order and allergies — and no longer than needed for tax records, which in [your state] generally means a few years for anything that counts as a sales record.

Ask us to delete your details and we will delete the messages and any note we have of you, keeping only what a tax authority requires us to keep.

Who else is involved

We use ordinary services to run a small business, and your information passes through some of them. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Our email provider, which stores the message you send
  • Instagram (Meta), if you order or ask questions by direct message
  • Cash App (Block) or Zelle (Early Warning Services), for payment
  • [your host], the service hosting these web pages, which keeps standard server logs — typically IP address, browser type and the time of the request — for security and reliability. We don't use those logs and don't combine them with anything else.

We don't hand your details to anyone beyond that, except where the law requires it — for example a lawful request from a public authority.

Allergy and dietary notes

Health information gets extra care. Anything you tell us about allergies or dietary needs is used only to answer your question honestly and to bake safely. It isn't shared with anyone, and if you'd rather it wasn't kept after your order, say so and it will be deleted while the rest of the order record stays.

Please do also read the allergen note in the FAQ: everything is made in a home kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, sesame and tree nuts, so no batch can be promised free of them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your details, beyond what tax records require
  • Stop contacting you altogether

Email the address at the bottom of this page and you'll get a reply. Depending on where you live, laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR may give you these rights formally; we'll honor the request either way and we won't treat you differently for asking. We have never sold personal information and have no plans to.

Children

This is a bakery, and children are very welcome to eat the bread. But the site isn't aimed at children and we don't knowingly collect details from anyone under 13. If a child has sent us their information, tell us and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If how we handle orders changes — a real checkout, a mailing list, a delivery service — this page gets updated and the date at the top changes with it. There is no archive of old versions; a small bakery's policy shouldn't need one.

Contact

Questions about any of this, or a request about your own details, go to our email address. We're a home kitchen in [your city, state], and a real person reads it.
