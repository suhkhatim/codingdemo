03

Method

Consultants who never leave aren’t partners, they’re payroll. Every engagement has a scope, a price and a finish line agreed before we start.

Typical duration Eleven to fourteen weeks for a full engagement, with a ninety-day check-in after handover.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Discover

    Two weeks of listening. Leadership interviews, a data pull from your HRIS, and a proper read of what your people are already telling you.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Diagnose

    An honest findings session — including the things nobody wants to say out loud — with the commercial cost of each issue quantified.

  3. Weeks 5–10

    Design

    We build the frameworks, documents and training with your team in the room, so ownership transfers as we go rather than in a handover meeting.

  4. Weeks 11+

    Deliver & embed

    Rollout, manager enablement, and a ninety-day check-in to make sure it stuck after we left the building.

05

Questions

The things people ask before they call. Answered plainly, in full.

Not covered? Ask directly — you’ll get a straight answer before you’ve spent a penny. Send a question.

How small is too small to work with you?
We regularly work with organisations from around twenty-five employees upward. Below that, a full engagement is usually poor value — we’ll tell you so, and point you at the two or three things worth doing yourself.
Do you replace our existing HR team?
Almost never. In most engagements the in-house team is capable but under-resourced or under-mandated. We add senior capacity, build alongside them, and hand over ownership. Making your team redundant isn’t a business model we’re interested in.
How do you charge?
Three models: fixed fee for scoped projects, a monthly retainer for fractional HR leadership, and day rates for casework and interim cover. You get the number before we start, and we don’t bill for the discovery call. PLACEHOLDER: add indicative fee ranges if you publish them.
Are you employment lawyers?
No — we’re HR practitioners, and we’re clear about the line. We handle strategy, process and risk management, and we work with your legal counsel, or introduce you to one, when a matter needs formal legal advice.
How quickly can you start?
Discovery calls usually happen within three working days. Project starts typically sit two to four weeks out, though we hold capacity for urgent employee relations matters — if it’s live and serious, say so and we’ll make room.
Do you work remotely or on site?
Both. Discovery and delivery work well remotely, but we’ll always be on site for leadership sessions, manager training, and anything involving sensitive conversations with employees.
