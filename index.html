Who we are

An HR partner that thinks like an operator, not a policy library.

S2 People Advisory was founded on a simple observation: most organisations don’t have an HR problem — they have a clarity problem. Roles that overlap, managers who were never taught to manage, reward that rewards the wrong things, and policies written for a company that no longer exists.

We come in, diagnose honestly, and build the people infrastructure your growth plan actually requires. No 90-slide decks nobody reads. No dependency on us forever. We hand over something your team can run.

  • People. We start with the humans in the org chart, not the boxes.
  • Performance. Every recommendation ties back to a commercial outcome.
  • Partnership. We work alongside your team, and we leave them stronger.

How we work

A four-stage engagement, with a defined end.

Consultants who never leave aren’t partners, they’re payroll. Every engagement has a scope, a price and a finish line agreed before we start.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Two weeks of listening. Leadership interviews, a data pull from your HRIS, a read of what your people are already telling you.

    Weeks 1–2
  2. 02

    Diagnose

    An honest findings session — including the things nobody wants to say out loud — with the commercial cost of each issue quantified.

    Weeks 3–4
  3. 03

    Design

    We build the frameworks, documents and training with your team in the room, so ownership transfers as we go.

    Weeks 5–10
  4. 04

    Deliver & Embed

    Rollout, manager enablement, and a 90-day check-in to make sure it stuck after we left the building.

    Weeks 11+
